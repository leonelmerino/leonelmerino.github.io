Tópicos en Ciencia de la Computación (IIC3800)

Graduate — Teaching

Description

This graduate seminar covers advanced and emerging topics in computer science, with a focus on human-computer interaction, software visualization, and immersive analytics. Students read and discuss recent research papers from top venues, critically evaluate methodologies, and develop a deep understanding of open problems in the field. The course fosters research skills including literature review, experimental design, and academic writing, preparing students to contribute original work to the computer science community.
