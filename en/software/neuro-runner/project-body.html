Description

Neuro Runner is an iOS side-scrolling game built with Swift and SpriteKit. It features animated player and zombie characters with multiple animation states — idle, walk, jump, and hurt — providing a fluid and engaging gameplay experience.

The project explores gamification as a research tool, with potential applications in neuroscience and human-computer interaction studies where engaging tasks are needed to sustain participant attention over time.
