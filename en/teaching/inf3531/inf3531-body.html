Visualización de Datos (INF3531)

Graduate — MTIG — Teaching

Description

This graduate course covers the principles and techniques of data visualization, focusing on how to communicate complex information through effective visual representations. Students study the perceptual and cognitive foundations that underpin good visualization design, including color theory, visual encoding, and interaction techniques. The course addresses both static and interactive visualizations, examining tools and frameworks for creating dashboards and exploratory visual analytics systems. Students apply these concepts through hands-on projects involving real datasets drawn from domains relevant to information technology management.
