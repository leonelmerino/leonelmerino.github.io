Diseño en VR/AR (DNO3289)

Graduate — MADA — Teaching

Description

This graduate studio course focuses on the design of experiences for virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) platforms. Students investigate how spatial computing reshapes the designer's practice, examining questions of presence, embodiment, and the blending of digital and physical worlds. The course combines theoretical readings on immersive media with hands-on prototyping using contemporary VR/AR authoring environments. Students produce design projects that critically engage with the possibilities and limitations of immersive technologies for artistic and professional applications.
