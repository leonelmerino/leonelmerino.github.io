Diseño en Realidad Extendida (DNO098)

Undergraduate — Teaching

Description

This undergraduate studio course introduces students to the design of experiences for extended reality (XR) environments, including virtual, augmented, and mixed reality. Students learn interaction design principles specific to immersive spatial computing, including three-dimensional user interface design, embodied interaction, and the challenges of designing for head-mounted displays and other XR devices. Through iterative prototyping and critique, students develop original XR projects that demonstrate both technical competency and thoughtful design reasoning.
