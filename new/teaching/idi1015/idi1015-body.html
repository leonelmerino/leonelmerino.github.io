Pensamiento Visual (IDI1015)

Undergraduate — Teaching

Description

This undergraduate course develops students' capacity to communicate ideas and reason through visual means. Students learn foundational principles of visual perception, graphic representation, diagramming, and information design to articulate complex concepts clearly and compellingly. The course covers sketching, mapping, charting, and visual narrative techniques applicable across design and engineering disciplines. Through studio exercises and critique sessions, students build a practical vocabulary of visual tools to support creative problem-solving and professional communication.
